Borrower Financial Review

An interactive reconciliation of fictional reported revenue, tax revenue, expenses, and debt service with a clearly limited coverage calculation.

PROJECT TYPEInteractive portfolio simulationDATA SOURCES100% fictional / generated examplesROLE FOCUSFinancial statement assessment / credit support
iDemonstration only. All company names, borrowers, figures, and records shown are fictional. These projects illustrate a review approach; they are not real employer records, underwriting decisions, or evidence of specific past transactions.
01 / THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Does the financial story reconcile?

A fictional service-business borrower has reported annual financial figures and submitted supporting tax information. The reviewer needs to identify variance between the two sources and describe what additional documentation could support a deeper credit evaluation.

FICTIONAL COMPANYHarborpoint Services LLC
REVIEW TYPEFinancial information reconciliation
This model uses a deliberately simplified cash-flow proxy (revenue less operating expenses) to explain a coverage ratio. It does not calculate lender-standard EBITDA, CFADS, or a policy-compliant DSCR.

Follow-up required
