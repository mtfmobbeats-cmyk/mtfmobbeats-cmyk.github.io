05 / REVIEW REASONING

How a flagged facility moves through review

  1. Define the population.

    Use all 24 synthetic facilities first, then isolate industries or watchlist groups to compare like with like.

  2. Recalculate under stress.

    Apply the visible scenario factors to cash available for debt service and annual payments; coverage is recomputed from those values rather than assigning unsupported default probabilities.

  3. Investigate exceptions.

    Negative headroom would prompt verification of source financials, covenant definitions, correct debt schedules, reporting periods, and any permitted adjustments or cures.

  4. Communicate specific next steps.

    Export the watchlist, prioritize potential exceptions by materiality and reporting deadline, and refer unresolved items to the appropriate credit or portfolio manager.
