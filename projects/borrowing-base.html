    04 / REVIEW NOTES

    How I would document this exception

    1. Reconcile the collateral source.

      Request aging reports and supporting schedules for receivables and inventory. Confirm whether aged, concentrated, disputed, or obsolete amounts should be excluded under the agreement.

    2. Separate eligible collateral from the gross balance.

      The sample begins with $980,000 gross A/R and $760,000 inventory. Exclusions are removed before applying the hypothetical 85% and 50% advance rates.

    3. Compare capacity with exposure.

      The default $938,500 net borrowing base less $780,000 outstanding leaves $158,500 in potential excess availability, below the requested $200,000 draw by $41,500.

    4. Recommend a documented next step.

      Flag the $41,500 shortfall; request a revised certificate, supporting collateral schedules, or an appropriately authorized exception review. Do not release funds from this simplified worksheet.

    RECRUITER TAKEAWAY

    Illustrates collateral eligibility, transparent borrowing-base formulas, exception triage, and disciplined referral. It is a self-created simulation, not a claim of prior ABL underwriting authority.

    What the project demonstrates

    Translate collateral schedules into an auditable borrowing-base bridge and a limited, supportable funding exception recommendation.
