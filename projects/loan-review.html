01 / THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

What needs attention before closing?

A fictional small-business borrower requests a $250,000 commercial loan. During document verification, an analyst must reconcile reported revenue, confirm supporting documents, and evaluate a lien search condition before referring the file for the appropriate next step.

    What this demonstrates

    Examine supporting records, detect inconsistencies, and communicate specific follow-up conditions. The outcome focuses on whether information is sufficiently documented for the next review stage.
